Kemitt speed and product audit

18 Aug 2026

Bitbucket kemitt-api, kemitt-website, kemitt-dashboard, and the Flutter client of api.kemitt.com / api.kemitt.sa. Code review of latency and conversion. No production traces.

The shop is slow because kemitt-api writes Redis for home/PDP/categories and never reads it, list aggregations join the whole catalog before pagination, and every product card re-queries offers. Flutter splash still waits on ~10 GraphQL calls. kemitt.com SSR fans out seven more. Furniture photos are original files on the CDN.
